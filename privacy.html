Privacy Policy — PulseScout AI

Effective Date: November 25, 2026 • Project Hub: PulseScout AI

PulseScout AI respects user privacy and operates with strict data minimization across its static web tools and social page automation.

1. Client-Side Web Calculator

The Position Size & Stop-Loss Calculator hosted on our web page executes 100% locally inside your browser using JavaScript. No inputs, calculations, cookies, or tracking identifiers are collected, stored, or transmitted to any server.

2. Public Market & Deal Feeds

PulseScout AI aggregates publicly available market ticker snapshots and public digital store discount listings for informational and educational purposes only. No personal financial advice or individual profiling is performed.

3. Meta / Facebook Page Integration

Our Meta application (PulseScout Poster) is used strictly by the page administrator to publish automated informational graphics on the official PulseScout Facebook Page and to reply directly when a user explicitly requests a link or information in a public comment on that page.

  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer any Facebook user data to third parties.
  • Only public comment identifiers already answered by our page are stored locally to prevent duplicate replies.

4. Data Deletion & Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or wish to request removal of a cached comment interaction identifier, please contact us at testtechteam200@gmail.com.
